Why I Built This

Every trip lived in six different places.

Gmail had the confirmations. Calendar had the dates. Notes had the packing list. Maps had the restaurants. Two separate airline apps had the boarding passes. None of them talked to each other, and I was the one reconciling all of it by hand, mid-trip, on a phone with bad wifi.

Trip OS is what I wanted instead: one layer that reads what's already landing in your inbox and calendar, keeps a single itinerary in sync as things change, and tells you the next right move instead of making you go find it. This is the first version, built around a real trip.

— Kyle Littlejohn, Founder
