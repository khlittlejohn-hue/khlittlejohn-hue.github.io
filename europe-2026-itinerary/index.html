Rec: Red-eye arrivals run 2+ hours of grogginess on average, so Day 1 is deliberately unscheduled except one walk and one dinner.

  • MorningSagrada Família (book timed entry ahead — this sells out)

Travel day
  • MorningLa Boqueria market, Passeig de Gràcia — Casa Batlló & Casa Milà exteriors
  • AfternoonFly Barcelona (BCN) → Palma de Mallorca (PMI)
  • EveningLand, transfer to the wedding hotel
Rec: Keep the morning unstructured — a delayed short-hop flight is the one thing that could actually threaten the wedding arrival.
