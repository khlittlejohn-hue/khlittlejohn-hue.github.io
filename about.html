The work

Five industries I knew nothing about

Biotech, credit ratings, health insurance, infrastructure SaaS, streaming media. I walked into each of them without the domain and had to become useful quickly, which turns out to be most of the job when you are the person handed the thing nobody owns.

That pattern is not on my resume as a skill, but it is probably the most transferable thing I do. The method in how I work came out of needing something that survives contact with an industry I have never seen.
