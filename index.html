How I work

Separate the auditor from the author.The agent that writes is never the one that grades. Optimism and skepticism belong in different seats.
A human on every decision that matters.Systems prepare and surface; people approve and send. Nothing autonomous reaches the outside world.
Make the invariants fail loud.Wrong numbers should crash the build, not ship quietly. Determinism where it counts.
Decompose, don't centralize.A legible system of single-purpose specialists beats one omniscient black box: easier to build, to trust, and to debug.
