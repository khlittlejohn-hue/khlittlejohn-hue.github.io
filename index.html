How I work

Working principles

  1. Find the real decision

    The presented problem and the real one usually differ. At Kaseya the symptom was incompatible KPI definitions; the problem was capital allocation across companies nobody could compare.

  2. Give leadership one basis for comparison

    Shared measures and named owners come before more reporting. You cannot allocate across a portfolio you cannot compare.

  3. Build the rhythm early

    Reporting and review cadence are operating infrastructure. Installed early, they surface risk while leadership still has cheap options.

  4. Inspect, then adjust

    The one who drafts is not the one who approves: separate reviewers check the work, and in my own system I approve it last. Recurring failures can become standing checks, and checks that stop earning their cost get retired.
