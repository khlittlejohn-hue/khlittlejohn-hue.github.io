The Operating System

Most AI workflows are a faster way to write a first draft.

Generation was never the constraint. Trust was. An assistant that is right most of the time, with no signal about which part is wrong, costs you the entire review.

So I built the layer that catches known classes of error automatically, and kept the human gate at the end for judgment. 120+ agents across 14 domains, and a quality layer that has grown one failure at a time.
