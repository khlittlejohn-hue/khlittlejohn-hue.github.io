01Challenge

16 business units, 12 partners, information in many places

AMC Networks was expanding its streaming business across a broad set of business units, adding channels, content categories and distribution partners. The work crossed 16 business units and 12 external partners, each with its own leaders and priorities, and status, risks and open decisions were spread across teams.

The practical cost showed up in where people spent their time. Teams were reconciling information and chasing status across groups when they could have been moving work forward.

02What I owned and did

The account, the team and the delivery work

The account and the team

  • I held Account Manager ownership of the account, built the operating cadence, and directly managed SOL Consulting’s program managers for 5+ years.
  • I interviewed and hired those program managers against a structured scorecard, onboarded and ramped them, and set their levels and career paths.
  • On the AMC Networks engagement, I managed a team of 8 consultants directly.
  • I directed engagement margin and delivery oversight on the account, covering pricing, consultant staffing mix, utilization and scope, reported against client and margin targets.
  • I coordinated the budget and headcount cases behind each renewal, from business rationale through approval with client finance.

Operating cadence and reporting

  • I built the intake system and the decision log, set the meeting cadence and decision forums across the 16 business units and 12 partners, and set up risk and dependency escalation.
  • I set the agendas and built the materials for the steering committees and leadership meetings.
  • I set up operational tracking in Airtable.
  • Late in the engagement I began applying ChatGPT and Microsoft Copilot to reporting workflows.

AMC+ launch

  • From 2020 to 2022, I contributed to the AMC+ launch and led change management across it.
  • I ran the cross-functional workshops across Product, Engineering, QA and Marketing that cleared launch blockers.
  • I coordinated user acceptance testing and release sequencing through go-live and cutover.

FAST and AVOD

  • From 2022 to 2026, as Senior Program Manager on the FAST and AVOD team, I stood up AMC’s FAST and AVOD lines of business.
  • I coordinated the launch calendar across all 32 FAST channels.
  • I coordinated the rollout of new partners and content categories across the FAST and AVOD portfolio.
03Decision

One way in for new partners

New partners and content categories kept arriving through the FAST and AVOD years. AMC’s leadership decided which ones to add, and I coordinated the rollout of each. Each addition could have been onboarded its own way, which is the easier path for any single partner.

I chose to define how additions would come into the existing operating model instead, so the portfolio took on new work through one process each time.

04Results

Results, with their basis and limits

  • The AMC Networks account grew from $280K to $2.1M a year across consecutive renewals, a 7.5x expansion over 5+ years, on an account I owned.
  • Roughly 30% shorter execution cycle time across 16 business units, from the intake system, decision log and operating cadence I built.

The account figures describe SOL Consulting’s account with AMC Networks across consecutive renewals, not AMC’s own revenue. The cycle-time figure is an approximate reduction across the 16 business units.

05What carries forward

Put the structure in early

Put the intake system, the decision log, the meeting cadence and the escalation path in place early, before execution problems make the case for them. They are easy to treat as administration that gets added once problems are visible. Put in early, they surface risk while leaders still have options.
